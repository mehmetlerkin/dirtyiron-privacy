Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Dirty Iron is a single-player game developed by Veyrox Games. In short: the game collects no personal data at all.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, email, location or any other personal information, has no accounts and does not connect to the internet.

Your saves

Your game progress, settings and achievements are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them. The optional backup code in the settings is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to put it.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The game contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that collect data. There are no in-app purchases. The slot machine and scratch cards in the game use in-game credits only and have no real-money value.

Sharing

If you tap "Share" on the weekly summary, your device's own share sheet opens and you decide where the text goes. Nothing is sent by us.

Children

The game is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For questions, use the developer contact shown on the game's store page.
